Venture funding flowing into Bangladeshi startups rose for the first time in six quarters, according to reporting in The Daily Star and Dhaka Tribune. Fintech and last-mile logistics accounted for the bulk of the increase.

> Both outlets cite a private quarterly snapshot, though neither discloses full deal values, which is why the item carries a *confirmed* rather than *fully verified* badge.

Observers caution that the rebound is concentrated in Dhaka and may not reflect the broader national picture.
